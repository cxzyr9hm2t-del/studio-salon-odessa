New Salon Build-Out

Designing the
Dream Salon

Converting a 28' x 32' double car garage into a stunning L-shaped salon. Complete plan, Ontario building codes, and cost breakdown for Summer 2026.

Proposed Layout
L-Shaped Floor Plan — 896 sq ft

Design Recommendations

Key elements for a trending 2026 salon design:

Reception & Waiting

Floating reception desk with LED accents. Seating for 4-6. Digital check-in tablet. Retail display wall for Redken. Beverage station.

4 Styling Stations

Hollywood-lit mirrors. Hydraulic chairs. Individual tool caddies. Built-in outlets at each station. Anti-fatigue mats.

2 Shampoo Bowls

Tilting porcelain bowls with neck-rest cushions. Hot water recirculation. Overhead storage. Ambient lighting.

Supply & Color Mix Room

Ventilated mixing station. Shelving for 200+ colors. Digital scale. Stainless countertops. Chemical-resistant surfaces.

ADA Bathroom

Grab bars, 60" turning radius. Floating vanity. Touchless fixtures. Humidity-sensor exhaust. Luxury vinyl floors.

Trending Elements

LED strip lighting. Terrazzo or polished concrete. Living plant wall. Arched mirrors. Purple & matte black accents. Bluetooth audio.

HVAC & Ventilation

2-ton split system. Chemical exhaust in mix room. Fresh air intake. Smart thermostat. Humidity control.

Electrical & Plumbing

200A panel upgrade. Dedicated circuits. 40-gal or tankless water heater. Backflow preventer. Floor drains at shampoo.

Laundry & Storage

Stackable washer/dryer. Towel storage. Broom closet. Back-stock shelving. Water heater access.
