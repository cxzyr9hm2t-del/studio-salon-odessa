Designing the Dream Salon

Converting a 28' x 32' double car garage into a stunning L-shaped salon. Here is our complete plan, building codes, and cost breakdown for opening Summer 2026.

L-Shaped Floor Plan — 896 sq ft

Design Recommendations

Our recommended layout maximizes the L-shape for workflow efficiency. Key recommendations for a trending 2026 salon design:

Reception & Waiting Area

Modern floating reception desk with LED accent lighting. Comfortable seating for 4-6 clients. Digital check-in tablet. Retail display wall for Redken products. Beverage station with mini-fridge.

4 Styling Stations

Full-length lit mirrors with Hollywood-style lighting. Hydraulic styling chairs. Individual tool caddies. Built-in outlets at each station. Anti-fatigue floor mats.

2 Shampoo Bowls

Tilting porcelain bowls with neck-rest cushions. Hot water recirculation system for instant hot water. Overhead storage for treatments. Ambient overhead lighting.

Supply & Color Mix Room

Dedicated color mixing station with ventilation. Organized shelving for 200+ color tubes. Digital scale for precise formulation. Stainless steel countertops. Chemical-resistant surfaces.

ADA-Accessible Bathroom

Fully accessible with grab bars, 60-inch turning radius. Modern floating vanity. Touchless fixtures. Exhaust fan with humidity sensor. Luxury vinyl tile flooring.

Laundry & Storage

Stackable washer/dryer unit. Towel storage and folding area. Broom closet. Water heater access. Organized shelving for back-stock and supplies.

Trending Design Elements

LED strip lighting under floating shelves. Terrazzo or polished concrete floors. Living plant wall / greenery accents. Arched mirrors. Warm purple and matte black accents. Bluetooth speaker system.

HVAC & Ventilation

Dedicated HVAC system (min 2-ton split system). Chemical fume exhaust in mix room. Fresh air intake. Smart thermostat. Humidity control to protect color products.

Electrical & Plumbing

200-amp service panel upgrade. Dedicated circuits for dryers and styling tools. Hot water heater (min 40-gal or tankless). Backflow preventer. Floor drains at shampoo stations.

Permits & Licenses Required

Building Permit

Required for structural changes, electrical, plumbing, HVAC. Submit plans to local building department. ~$500-$2,000 depending on scope.

Zoning Approval

Confirm home-based business or commercial zoning allows a salon. May need variance or minor variance application through Loyalist Township.

Health & Safety

Public health unit inspection for sanitation compliance. Proper disinfection stations, clean water supply, waste disposal protocols.

Business License

Municipal business license. HST registration if revenue exceeds $30K. Liability insurance ($2M minimum recommended). WSIB if hiring.
